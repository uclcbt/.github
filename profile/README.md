The UCL CBT was founded in 2015 to research the effects of Distributed Ledger Technologies (DLT) and Blockchain on our socio-economic systems and to promote the safe and organic development and adoption of Blockchain-based platforms. The centre is the nucleus for DLT and Blockchain research and engagement across multiple departments in UCL and for its Research and Industry Associate network. Our Research and Industry Associate community consists of over two hundred researchers and practitioners from UCL, other academic institutions and companies utilising DLT.
