# UCL Centre for Blockchain Technologies (CBT)

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the UCL Centre for Blockchain Technologies!

## About Us

The UCL Centre for Blockchain Technologies (CBT) is a leading academic research centre at University College London, dedicated to advancing the understanding and development of blockchain and distributed ledger technologies.

## Our Focus

We conduct cutting-edge research in:

- **Blockchain Architecture & Scalability**: Exploring novel consensus mechanisms and distributed system designs
- **Cryptography & Security**: Advancing cryptographic protocols and security analysis
- **Smart Contracts & DeFi**: Researching decentralized finance, tokenomics, and programmable money
- **Privacy & Identity**: Developing privacy-preserving technologies and digital identity solutions
- **Applications & Use Cases**: Investigating blockchain applications across industries
